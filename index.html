Accounting edge cases and the quant feedback loop

Making accounting match upstream behavior

Corrected conservation arithmetic to accept valid negative intermediate values, and adjusted harvest accounting to reflect SOL already returned by the upstream program. Unpaid seller claims remain attributed across capped payouts and later realization.

The keeper distinguishes an absent token account from an RPC failure. An unmeasurable balance takes a refusal and reporting path rather than being treated as zero.

From quant specifications to observable execution

In our three-person team, I implement the quant co-founder's models, reviews and execution requirements. Built versioned decision, settlement, cost and reconciliation telemetry, with 39 days of archived core exports for review and diagnosis.

Mining, Staking and Protocol are different pool roles. The beta is deployed on Solana mainnet; the archive demonstrates traceability, not uninterrupted uptime or investment performance.
