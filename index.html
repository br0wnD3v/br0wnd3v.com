  • The product around the protocol. Shipped synthetic-market trading and liquidity flows, funding/revenue APIs and a reconciled referral-payout state machine. Hardened API authorization and added admin audit logging.
  • Leadership and release responsibility. Led one engineer in a five-person team. Worked directly with both founders, including the CTO, and coordinated the quant's requirements with implementation and delivery.
Market expansion, oracle reliability and governed releases

Five synthetic markets, with real trading hours

Launched gold, silver, WTI, S&P 500 and NVDA perpetuals across contracts, market-hours services and the trading UI, including liquidity mint/redeem flows. Signed attestations gated trading when an underlying market was closed.

The oracle pipeline recorded roughly 94,000 signed price batches per day over an eight-week window. I split price writes from AUM consensus so a provider outage would not repeatedly revert the fresh writes. Versioned transactions and address lookup tables brought oversized oracle transactions within Solana's packet limit. RPC failover was designed around the protocol's seven-second freshness budget.

Audit remediation through governed releases

Triaged 33 Fidesium findings and patched 15, including a two-step admin handover with a 48-hour timelock. Authored the remediation report and documented verifiable builds released through DAO governance.

Volume and trader figures describe platform activity during my tenure, not growth attributed to me. Batch throughput is an estimate from database sequence deltas, not a count of confirmed on-chain transactions. Adrena wound down trading in 2026.

02 · TERMINAL & MONEY PATHS

NABLA

Sole engineerSep 2026 - Present · Live on mainnet

Product ↗DefiLlama adapter ↗
1engineer, end to end
2 daysfrom first commit to production

Built and run a live Solana trading terminal that shows how each token rewards its holders.

  • Money paths that reconcile. Swaps route through Jupiter with a 0.50% platform fee, settled in SOL, USDC or USDT, into referral fee accounts and a 70/15/15 treasury split. A separate checker matches fees and balances against finalized mainnet transactions.
  • Security on the live app. Ran an adversarial security audit: no critical issues, and all four high-severity findings fixed in production the same day. Ran another security pass on new routes later.
More detail and system architecture
  • Reward maps from chain data. Decoded launchpad holder-reward payouts from finalized transactions to build and check the reward trees the terminal shows.
  • Web and Android. One public API serves the Next.js terminal and an Android app with Mobile Wallet Adapter swaps, ready to ship to the Solana dApp Store on Seeker. NABLA's fee and revenue adapter is merged into DefiLlama.
How the money paths are checked

Proof on mainnet

Eight authorized mainnet transactions finalized: fee-account setup, six swaps and one referral claim. Every fee reconciles as floor(base × 50 / 10,000), and the checker confirms the split, wallet balances and minimum receipts.

How we split the work

Zedef leads product direction and makes the calls. I build, test, audit and run it, so every release, from the terminal to the Android app, goes through one pair of hands.

Live on Solana mainnet at nabladefi.com. The security audit was our own adversarial review, not a third-party audit.
